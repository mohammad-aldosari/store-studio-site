Quick questions

Do you take a commission on my sales?

No — 0% commission. You only pay the setup and subscription. The payment company has its own fees, and the money goes straight to your account.

Who owns the store and the data?

Your products, orders and customer data are yours, and the domain is registered in your name. The design and system are ours and you use them while subscribed; if you leave, we hand over your data and photos.

Do I need a licence?

Yes. We need a copy of a valid licence (home-business licence or CR) before we start. Some products, like food, may need extra approvals.

When do online payments start working?

We set it up within the 5 days, but activation depends on the payment company’s approval. Until then you can take cash on delivery or a payment link.

Can I cancel?

Yes, any time. It takes effect at the end of the period you paid for. Details in the Refund & Cancellation policy.
