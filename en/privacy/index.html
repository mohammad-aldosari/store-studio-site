Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026 · Version 1.1

We respect your privacy and collect as little data as possible, in line with Law No. 13 of 2016 on Personal Data Privacy Protection.

Data controller

Store Studio (Mohammad Aldosari), Doha, Qatar, email: qdropai@gmail.com.

Data we collect

  • What you type into the order/consultation form: name, mobile number, business type, Instagram link and notes.
  • This data is not stored on the website’s servers; on submit it is passed to WhatsApp on your device and reaches us as a message.
  • We never ask for or collect card details or passwords through the site.
  • Your language preference and cookie choice are stored only in your browser (localStorage).

Why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry, prepare a quote and deliver the service.
  • To issue invoices and keep a record of agreement acceptance.
  • We do not sell your data or share it with anyone for marketing.

Cookies and analytics

The site uses only necessary storage to remember your language and cookie choice. Analytics tools such as Google Analytics and Meta Pixel run only after you accept them in the cookie banner; you can withdraw consent by clearing the site’s data in your browser.

Third parties

  • WhatsApp (Meta) to receive messages.
  • The site’s hosting provider, which may log technical data such as IP addresses for security.
  • Google and Meta only if you accept analytics.

Retention

We keep order correspondence, invoices and agreement acceptance for the duration of our relationship and afterwards as required by law, and delete anything else on request.

Your rights

  • Access your data and request a copy.
  • Correct or delete it.
  • Withdraw consent at any time.
  • To exercise any right, email qdropai@gmail.com.

Security

We use strong passwords, two-factor authentication and backups, and notify affected people and the authorities of any breach as required by law.

Our clients’ customers

In the stores we build, the store owner is responsible for their customers’ data; we process it on their instructions only and never use it for any other purpose.
