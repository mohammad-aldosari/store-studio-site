Service Agreement

Last updated: 25 October 2026 · Version 1.1

This agreement is between the “Provider” and the “Client” and must be accepted electronically before work begins. We record the date and time of acceptance and a copy of the agreement and send it to the client. The Arabic version prevails.

1. Parties

“Provider”: Store Studio, owned by Mohammad Aldosari, home-business licence no. Pending issuance. “Client”: the store owner ordering the service.

2. Scope of service

  • Only what the selected package includes (Starter, Store or Pro) per the package table published on the site; any request outside it is quoted separately.
  • The Client uploads their own products, images and videos from the dashboard; the Provider delivers an Arabic PDF guide to using the dashboard.
  • Two design revision rounds before launch.

3. Delivery time and Client obligations

  • Delivery is 5 working days from receipt of the first payment and all content.
  • Required content: logo, categories, 5 sample products, contact details, a copy of the licence or CR, and a payment-gateway account in the Client’s name (for packages that include it).
  • Delays by the Client in providing content or replying pause the count.
  • If the Provider is late, the first month’s subscription is free.
  • Payment-gateway activation depends on the gateway’s own approval and is not part of the 5 days.

4. Storage

  • Storage limit per package: 1, 3 or 10 GB, with the file-size limits published on the site.
  • When full, uploads stop automatically without deleting any content; the Client may buy an extra 5 GB for QAR 15/month or upgrade.

5. Payment and renewal

  • Setup: 50% on acceptance and 50% before launch.
  • The monthly or yearly subscription is paid in advance and renews automatically, with an Arabic invoice for each payment.
  • The optional “3 years” plan: one advance payment covering setup and a 36-month subscription for the stated package; it does not renew automatically, and at the end the Client chooses to renew at the then-published prices or cancel.
  • Late payment: reminder after 7 days, temporary suspension of the store after 14 days, data kept for 30 days.

6. Cancellation and refunds

  • Before work starts: full refund.
  • After design starts: the first payment is non-refundable, unless the service does not match what was agreed, in which case the work is redone or the amount refunded under Consumer Protection Law No. 8 of 2008.
  • Subscription: may be cancelled at any time, effective at the end of the paid period, with no partial refunds.

7. Client responsibility for their store

  • The store’s content, products, prices, descriptions and their accuracy.
  • Owning the rights to images, marks and text, or having permission to use them.
  • A valid licence for their activity and any approvals required for their products.
  • Their customer service, delivery, exchanges and returns, and issuing Arabic invoices in QAR to their customers.
  • A privacy policy for their store and protecting their customers’ data as the party responsible for it.
  • Obtaining the required licence for any promotions or discounts in their store.
  • Not selling prohibited or counterfeit products (see the Prohibited Content policy).

8. Provider responsibility and limits

  • Fixing software bugs free of charge throughout the subscription.
  • Maximum liability is the amount paid by the Client in the last 3 months, with no liability for lost profits, to the extent permitted by law.
  • No uptime guarantee beyond what the hosting provider guarantees, and no liability for outages of third-party services such as payment gateways, WhatsApp and Instagram.
  • No promises of sales or search-engine rankings.

9. Data ownership and exit

  • The Client owns their content and the data of their products, orders and customers; the domain is registered in the Client’s name.
  • The Provider owns the template and code; the Client may use them during the subscription.
  • On exit: export of products, orders and customers (CSV) and images (ZIP) within 14 days, domain transfer, then data deletion after 30 days.

10. Suspension for content

The Provider may remove any content that violates the law, public morals or third-party rights, or suspend the store as soon as it becomes aware of it, without refund.

11. Store customers’ data

The Provider acts as a data processor on the Client’s instructions only, does not use the data for any other purpose, and notifies the Client immediately of any breach.

12. Account handover

The Client receives the admin account and is responsible for its password; the Provider keeps technical access for maintenance only. The payment-gateway account is in the Client’s name and funds go directly to the Client.

13. Changes to terms

With 30 days’ prior notice; the Client may cancel if they do not agree.

14. Governing law

Qatari law, the competent courts in Doha, and the Arabic text prevails.

15. Acceptance

Ticking the box “I have read and agree to the Terms & Conditions and the Service Agreement” constitutes electronic acceptance of this agreement. We record the date, time and version and send a copy to the Client by email or WhatsApp.
