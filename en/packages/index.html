What we do, what you do

Clear from day one.

We do 🛠️

  • Design and set up the store for your package
  • Connect payments, order receiving and delivery
  • Run, protect and back up your store
  • Fix any system bug free during your subscription
  • Give you an illustrated Arabic guide
  • Monthly edits by package

You do 📸

  • Logo, categories, 5 products and contact details
  • A copy of your licence or CR
  • Payment-company account and domain in your name (their fees are yours)

  • Customer service, delivery and exchanges
  • Accuracy of your content and rights to your photos

How do you pay us?

Setup: half when we agree, half before launch. The subscription is paid in advance (monthly, yearly or 3 years), with an Arabic invoice in QAR for every payment.

More questions

How many changes can I ask for?

Two design revision rounds before launch, then small monthly edits by package: 1, 3 or unlimited.

How do I use the dashboard?

You get an illustrated step-by-step Arabic guide with your store, and you upload products and photos yourself.

What if my storage is full?

Uploads pause; nothing is deleted. We can add space for QAR 15/month, or you upgrade.

Who owns the domain (web address)?

On Store and Pro we connect a web address with your store’s name, registered to you. Its yearly fee is paid directly to the registrar.

Do you build stores for any product?

No. We don’t build stores for prohibited, counterfeit or unlawful products. See the Prohibited Content policy.
