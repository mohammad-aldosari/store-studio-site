Prohibited Content

Last updated: 25 October 2026 · Version 1.1

We do not build or host stores or content that violate Qatari law or public morals. This list is illustrative, not exhaustive.

  • Terms & Conditions
  • Service Agreement
  • Privacy Policy
  • Refund & Cancellation
  • Prohibited Content

Prohibited products and content

  • Counterfeit goods or goods infringing trademarks or copyright.
  • Alcohol, drugs, e-cigarettes and substances banned in Qatar.
  • Weapons, ammunition and hazardous materials.
  • Medicines and supplements requiring a licence, without that licence.
  • Pornographic content or content offending public morals.
  • Gambling and betting.
  • Content insulting religions or inciting hatred or violence.
  • Fraud, pyramid schemes and misleading products.
  • Selling personal data, accounts or hacking tools.
  • Any product requiring government approval without obtaining it.

Images and text

  • Do not use images, fonts or text you do not own or have permission to use.
  • Product descriptions and prices must be accurate and not misleading.
  • Promotions and discounts require the necessary licence from the Ministry of Commerce and Industry.

What happens on violation

We may remove violating content or suspend the store as soon as we become aware of it, without refund, and may report to the authorities where required.

Reporting

If you notice violating content in a store we host, contact us at qdropai@gmail.com or WhatsApp +974 5025 5527.
