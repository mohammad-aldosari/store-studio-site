Refund & Cancellation

Last updated: 25 October 2026 · Version 1.1

A clear cancellation and refund policy that complements the Service Agreement.

Setup fees

  • Before work starts: full refund of the first payment.
  • After design starts: the first payment (50%) is non-refundable.
  • If the delivered service does not match what we agreed, we redo the work at no charge or refund the amount, under Consumer Protection Law No. 8 of 2008.

Delivery guarantee

If we do not deliver within 5 working days of receiving the first payment and all content, the first month’s subscription is free. This excludes delays caused by the client or waiting for payment-gateway approval.

Monthly, yearly or 3-year subscription

  • May be cancelled at any time by WhatsApp or email.
  • Cancellation takes effect at the end of the paid period, with no partial refund for the remaining period (this includes the prepaid 3-year plan).
  • After cancellation we hand over your data (CSV) and images (ZIP) within 14 days and transfer the domain, then delete the data after 30 days.

Late payment

  • Reminder 7 days after the due date.
  • Temporary suspension of the store after 14 days.
  • Data kept for 30 days before deletion.

Add-ons

Add-ons are fully refundable only before work on them begins.

Third-party fees

Domain and payment-gateway fees are subject to their providers’ policies and cannot be refunded by us.

Suspension for violations

Suspension of a store for content that breaches the Prohibited Content policy does not entitle the client to any refund.
